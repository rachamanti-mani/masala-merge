About Masala Merge

Masala Merge is a free browser puzzle game created by Mr.MR. Swipe to slide ingredients across a 4×4 board. When two identical ingredients touch they merge into the next one, from the humble chilli all the way up to the Royal Thali.

Quick facts
• Free to play, with no download or sign-up
• Works on phones, tablets and computers
• Controls: swipe, drag, arrow keys, W/A/S/D or on-screen buttons
• Your best score is saved on your own device

Why we made it

We wanted a simple, relaxing puzzle with an Indian food theme that anyone can pick up in seconds. New features such as a daily challenge and more games are planned.
