Masala Merge Strategy Guide

Want to reach the Royal Thali? This guide covers how scoring works, the ingredient chain, and the habits that help you score higher in Masala Merge.

The ingredient chain

🌶️ Chilli → 🧄 Garlic → 🧅 Onion → 🍅 Tomato → 🥔 Potato → 🥕 Carrot → 🍆 Brinjal → 🍚 Rice → 🍛 Curry → 🍲 Dal Pot → 🏆 Royal Thali

Two identical ingredients merge into the next one in the chain. New ingredients appear after every move, usually a chilli and sometimes a garlic.

How scoring works

Each merge scores the points of the ingredient it creates:
Garlic 4 · Onion 8 · Tomato 16 · Potato 32 · Carrot 64 · Brinjal 128 · Rice 256 · Curry 512 · Dal Pot 1,024 · Royal Thali 2,048

Because points double at every step, one big merge is worth far more than many small ones. Protect your biggest ingredient.

Five habits of high scorers

1. Pick a corner. Keep your biggest ingredient in one corner and never move it out unless you have no other move.

2. Use only three directions. If your corner is bottom-left, swipe mostly left and down. Use right only when you must, and use up as a last resort.

3. Build a ladder. Line up ingredients along the edge from biggest to smallest, so each merge triggers the next.

4. Keep the board open. Merge small ingredients early. A crowded board leaves you with no safe moves.

5. Think before you swipe. Check where the new ingredient will land and which merge you want next.

Common mistakes

Swiping randomly, chasing small merges while a big ingredient is stuck in the middle, and moving the biggest ingredient out of its corner. If that happens, rebuild the ladder slowly instead of panicking.

Frequently asked questions

Is Masala Merge free? Yes. There is nothing to download and no sign-up.

Is my score saved? Your best score is saved in your browser on your own device.

How many chillies make a Royal Thali? The equivalent of 1,024 chillies, which is why planning matters.

Can I keep playing after the Royal Thali? Yes. You can keep going to push your score higher.
