How to play

Swipe on your phone or use the arrow keys (or W, A, S, D) on a computer. Every move slides all ingredients in that direction. When two identical ingredients touch, they merge into the next one. Keep merging to climb the chain, and try to create the Royal Thali before the board fills up.

Tips to get a high score

Keep your biggest ingredient in a corner and build the chain next to it. Avoid swiping in a direction that pulls it out of the corner. Plan two moves ahead, and use new games to practise your merging pattern. A calm, steady strategy beats fast swiping. Want more? Read the full Masala Merge strategy guide.

About Masala Merge

Masala Merge is a free browser puzzle game inspired by classic merge games, with a food theme. It runs on any phone, tablet or computer with no download or sign-up. Your best score is saved on your device. Created by Mr.MR.
